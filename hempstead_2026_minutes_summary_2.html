Patterns Worth Watching

  • ▶ Tax certiorari settlements at nearly every BOT meeting. Each refund locks in a lower prospective assessment for 3 future years, permanently reducing tax revenue. Confirmed 2026 refunds across reviewed meetings: $146,300 (24-26 Main St $130K · Go Equities $3.3K · Peninsula Realty $13K) plus prior meetings totaling $225,290 — grand running total exceeds $371,000 in refunds plus cascading assessment cuts.
  • ▶ Workers' Comp settlements approved without recipient or amount — now 3 confirmed instances. Apr 21: no amount, no recipient. Jul 21: no amount, no recipient (due Jul 24). Aug 25: $108,235.22, no recipient. Pattern is consistent across all three.
  • ▶ Executive session followed immediately by undisclosed litigation votes — now 4 instances, 8 total "matter" approvals. Feb 17: Matter 1 + 2. May 19: Matter 1 + 2. Aug 3: Matter 1 + 2. Aug 18: 1 matter. None carry public amounts or party names. This is now a structural pattern.
  • ▶ Trustee Burroughs attends via Zoom regularly — confirmed on Jan 20 (arrived late), Mar 3 (in person), May 19, Jun 2, Jul 7. Was fully absent Mar 17. His Zoom access for the public on Aug 25 was never opened despite being advertised.
  • ▶ Alexis Grocery Deli 6 Corp (76 N. Franklin) — resolved Jun 16 after 4 adjournments. License reinstated via stipulation. The Jun 16 vote shows only Burroughs, Whitaker, and Mayor Hobbs in AYES — Daniels seconded but is not listed; Carter not listed. May be a clerical error or an actual 3-2 split.
  • ▶ 330 Fulton Ave — no acquisition vote found. $5,000 appraisal authorized Feb 3 for "contemplated acquisition." No follow-up resolution appears in any subsequent meeting through Aug 18.
  • ▶ Hempstead Preservation LLC — no follow-up activity found. Received a Village Services agreement (no amount) and $500,000 Contribution Pledge in January. Neither appears in any meeting Jan 20 – Aug 18.
  • ▶ Undisclosed consulting contracts are a recurring pattern. Greenberg Traurig LLP for lobbying (Mar 3, no amount). Crescent Consulting 5-year referral program ($519,936, no competitive bid mentioned). Prioleau Development Group +$10,000/month for Capital Projects (Jul 7, no amount or scope disclosed). McBride Consulting $7,500/month for Capital Projects (Jul 21, no competitive bid). Laberge Group receives repeated addenda with no competitive rebid.
  • ▶ Residency waivers routine and increasing. Cumulative 2026 waivers confirmed: Johnny Izzo, Christopher Reid, Joselyn Ortiz-Reyes, Isys Morris, 7 seasonal lifeguards (May 19), Karandeep Singh, Justin Elder, Natividad Calderon-Valle, Mayra Vargas, 3 more lifeguards (Jun 16). At minimum 17 individual waivers to date.
  • ▶ Village partially opted back into NY adult-use cannabis retail program — reversing Local Law 1 of 2022. Public hearings Jun 18 and Jun 22 closed without recorded public testimony. Ordinance vote not yet confirmed in posted minutes.
  • ▶ Kennedy Park Pool — $17.4M renovation contracts awarded Aug 18. Renu Contracting & Restoration received the general contract ($10,482,994). Trustee Burroughs abstained on that specific vote — no reason stated in minutes.
  • ▶ Planning Board Chairperson Kennetha Pettus absent from 3 consecutive meetings (Feb 26, Mar 16, Apr 20). Meetings proceeded without her. She returned May 18.
  • ▶ Village Hall relocation to Helen Keller Building is now active. H2M master planning authorized Mar 17 ($90,000). H2M 3rd floor renovation authorized Mar 3 (no amount). Olive Dove Design Collective addendum authorized Jan 20 (no amount). This is a major capital initiative with limited public disclosure of total cost.
